## Demo 2: From Meeting to Management Update

### Prompts for Copilot in Teams
>Help me prepare for this meeting

>Suggest 5 icebreaking questions

>Catch me up on the meeting so far

>Who have participated in the discussion? Summarize what each person has discussed.

>What different views have participants expressed?

>What questions or issues have not yet been resolved?

>What assumptions are we making in this discussion?

>Tabulate all the action items, owners and deadline

>Using the meeting information, draft a management update. Structure it as:
>1. Purpose
>2. Key observations
>3. Decisions
>4. Agreed actions
>5. Next steps

### Prompts for Copilot in Word
>Transform these meeting minutes into an executive-ready summary for senior management. Focus on outcomes, decisions, business impact, risks, management attention required, and key actions. Remove operational detail and repetition. Keep the Executive Summary to no more than five bullets, and do not add information that is not supported by the original minutes.

>Review the doc, suggest areas of improvement to make it more executive ready
